## What this company does

Rx Redefined replaces the third-party DMEPOS supplier in the prescription-to-patient path. A
practice is onboarded and licensed with CMS as a physician-owned DMEPOS supplier, then orders
supplies electronically through the Rx Redefined application. Each order is checked against
DMEPOS compliance rules, medical billing requirements and patient eligibility before it is
fulfilled from a network of 10+ U.S. distribution centers. Rx Redefined also runs the
revenue cycle and audit management behind it.

Two products:

- **Full-Service** — https://rxredefined.com/platform/full-service — the physician personally
  performs the DMEPOS supplier standards; the platform documents that personal performance
  per order.
- **In-Office** — https://rxredefined.com/platform/in-office — distribution from the practice's
  own offices under the in-office ancillary services exception, with delivery to the office
  and patient pick-up.

Reported reach: 350+ U.S. clinics, millions of products distributed annually.

The platform integrates with EMR systems and with distribution-center supply chains, but those

## Compliance posture

- HIPAA — self-asserted on the homepage ("meet all required and addressable HIPAA regulations").
- ISO 27001 — claimed for the *hosting infrastructure* the platform runs on, not for the company.
- CMS DMEPOS Supplier Standards (42 CFR 424.57) and the Stark Law in-office ancillary services
  exception are the domain standards this product is built around; both are cited in prose on
  the product pages, neither is declared in a machine-readable contract.
- No security.txt, no vulnerability disclosure program, no bug bounty, no trust center
  (all probed 2026-08-26 — no hits).
- Domain security (probed): TLS 1.3 on rxredefined.com, TLS 1.2 on app.rxredefined.com; SPF and
  DMARC present (`p=none`); no HSTS, no DNSSEC, no CAA records.
